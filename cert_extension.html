Indian Certificates expiration extended to Dec 2027

Due to the ongoing conflicts between Israel, Iran, Saudi Arabia and the United States of America. Prime Minister Narendra Modi has extended all financial certificates.
All certificates will receive an extension through Dec 31 2027 effective immediately.
The current world circumstances have created great volatility and unrest for Indian property owners. PM Modi has determined an extension for all financial certificates to be the most fair outcome. No penalties will be allowed and all certificate holders will accumulate interest at the contracted rate compounded annually. Many will receive interest between 15-20%. This interest is guaranteed by the Indian Goverment.
PM Modi advises for all certficates to be redeemed in December 2027 as they will receive the complete full interest payments. However, please note, any attempt at retrieving certificates before December 2027 will result in forfeiture of all accrued interest. Unspecified penalties may arise with premature redemption attempts.
