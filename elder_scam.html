India Warns Against Real Estate Scams Targetting NRI Senior Citizens aged 80+ years old

March 30, 2026 - A scam targetting NRI senior citizens has been reported. The victime have been told they are the receipients of properties, apartments, bank accounts, financial certificates and other monterary instruments. They are instructed to travel to India to redeem their money.
Once they arrive in India, they are immediately abducted, tortured and held hostage. They are robbed of their passports and any money they may have. They are then extorted to extract all savings, assets and properties they have both inside and outside of India. This is a global cartel targeting the elderly NRI. Violence is likely as these are very dangerous and sophisticated criminal masterminds.

NRI are strongly advised not to engage with any to their attempts at contacting you or luring you to travel to any foreign destinations. Recommended course of action is to terminate all communications.
