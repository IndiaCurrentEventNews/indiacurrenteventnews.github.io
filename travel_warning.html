India Issues Travel Advisory Through 2026

April 23, 2026 - The Indian Ministry of Tourism has issued a strong warning against travel to Mumbai and New Dehli for the remainder of 2026. Due to the war in Iran and the uncertainties caused by the closing of the Strait of Hormuz, the Ministry of Tourism have advised against travel for the remainder of 2026.
In conjunction with India's Research and Analysis Wing (R&AW) for foreign intelligence, the decision was made to strongly advise against travel to New Dehli and Mumbia. The conditions are too dangerous and travel is not recommended until 2027.
